# LinkedIn AI Posts — System

## How It Works
1. Claude finds news → writes 3 posts → saves to `/posts/` folder
2. You review and edit if needed
3. You copy and post to LinkedIn

## Post Format
Each post file contains:
- The story/angle
- The humanised LinkedIn post (ready to copy-paste)
- Source links

## Voice & Tone
- Graeme Wallace — Head of Digital Transformation perspective
- Straight-talking, not hype. Show the reality.
- First-person observations, not press releases
- Hook → Story → Insight → Question/CTA
- No corporate waffle. No emojis overdose (1-2 max).
- 150-300 words. Punchy.

## Topic Mix (rotate)
1. Where AI went wrong (cautionary tales)
2. Real ROI wins (specific numbers, not vague)
3. The human side (jobs, culture, change management)
4. Practical how-tos / things I've learned
5. Industry transformation observations

## Naming Convention
posts/YYYY-MM-DD_slug.md
