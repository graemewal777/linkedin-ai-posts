# This Week in Business AI — Weekly Brief Template

## Format
One roundup post per week covering 3-5 stories. Published as a single LinkedIn post.
Tone: "here's what I noticed this week" — curator + commentator, not journalist.

---

## LinkedIn Post Structure

**Hook:** One punchy observation from the week

**Story 1 — The Win:** A real ROI story or use case with numbers
**Story 2 — The Warning:** Something that went wrong or a reality check
**Story 3 — The Trend:** A pattern or shift worth paying attention to
*(Optional Story 4 — The Copilot/Enterprise angle)*

**Closing take:** Your 2-sentence view on what it all means
**CTA:** One question to the audience

---

## Example Post Shell

This week in business AI — here's what stood out:

**✅ THE WIN**
[Company] did [X] with AI and got [specific result]. The reason it worked: [one-line insight].

**⚠️ THE WARNING**
[Company/study] showed [what went wrong]. The lesson: [one-line takeaway].

**📈 THE TREND**
[Pattern observed]. If you're not thinking about [X] yet, you're already behind.
