# POST 2: 95% of AI Pilots Never Scale — The Uncomfortable Truth

## The Story
MIT data shows 95% of generative AI pilots never scale. S&P Global found 42% of companies abandoned AI initiatives in 2025, up from 17% the year before. The problem isn't the technology — it's the approach.

---

## LinkedIn Post (copy-paste ready)

95% of AI pilots never scale.

That's MIT's number. And it tracks with what I'm seeing.

Everyone's running pilots. Proof of concepts. "Innovation sprints." Demos that impress the board.

Then... nothing. The initiative quietly dies. The vendor gets replaced. Someone else's budget gets blamed.

Here's the pattern I keep seeing:

**The pilot solves the wrong problem.**
Teams pick the easy win — the use case that demos well — not the one that actually matters to the business.

**Nobody owns the outcome.**
AI projects with no named business owner fail 56% of the time within six months. Not a technology problem. A sponsorship problem.

**The business case was built backwards.**
"We bought the AI, now let's find where to use it." That's not transformation. That's expensive box-ticking.

S&P Global found 42% of companies abandoned AI initiatives in 2025. Up from 17% the year before.

That's not a technology crisis. That's a strategy crisis.

The companies actually seeing returns? They started with the business problem — not the tool. They assigned an owner. They measured against a real outcome.

Technology is 20% of the challenge. People, process and culture is the other 80%.

Same as it ever was.
