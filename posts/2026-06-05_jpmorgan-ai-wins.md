# POST 3: JPMorgan — $1.5 Billion in Savings. This Is What Real AI ROI Looks Like.

## The Story
JPMorgan Chase used AI to prevent $1.5 billion in fraud losses. Their system detects fraud 300x faster than traditional methods, with 98% accuracy. This is what a properly scoped, high-volume, rules-based AI use case looks like when done right.

---

## LinkedIn Post (copy-paste ready)

JPMorgan saved $1.5 billion using AI.

Not a pilot. Not a proof of concept. Real money. Real results.

Here's why it worked — and what most AI projects miss by comparison:

They didn't pick a flashy use case. They picked the **right** one.

Fraud detection is:
✓ High volume (millions of transactions daily)
✓ Rules-based (clear right/wrong outcomes)
✓ Measurable (dollars saved, false positives reduced)
✓ Fast feedback loop (you know immediately if it's working)

Their AI now detects fraud 300x faster than the old system. 98% accuracy. $1.5 billion in losses prevented.

That's not magic. That's matching the technology to the problem it's actually suited for.

Compare that to the companies I see deploying AI in boardrooms to summarise PowerPoints. Same technology. Completely different outcome.

The question isn't "how do we use AI?"

It's "where does AI have an unfair advantage over what we're doing today?"

High-volume. Repetitive. Pattern-based. Clear success criteria.

Find that in your business first. That's where you start.

Everything else is a distraction.
