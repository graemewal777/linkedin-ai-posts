# POST 1: Klarna — The AI Cautionary Tale Every Board Is Now Quoting

## The Story
Klarna fired 700 customer service workers and replaced them with an AI agent. They announced it proudly. The numbers looked great on paper. Then they started hiring humans again.

---

## LinkedIn Post (copy-paste ready)

Klarna replaced 700 customer service workers with AI.

Then quietly started hiring humans again.

Here's what actually happened — and why it matters for every business considering AI workforce changes:

**The headline:** AI handled the volume. Costs dropped. Leadership looked like geniuses.

**The reality:** Customer satisfaction tanked. Edge cases, upset customers, complex problems — the AI just couldn't handle them. It's trained for routine. Life isn't routine.

By early 2026, Klarna shifted to a hybrid model. AI handles the straightforward stuff. Humans handle everything else.

The lesson isn't "AI doesn't work." The lesson is more uncomfortable than that.

They optimised for cost before they understood what customers actually needed from a conversation.

I've seen this pattern in digital transformation projects for years — it's just faster and more public with AI.

The technology works. The strategy was wrong.

Before you cut headcount, ask: what are we actually optimising for? Cost per interaction, or customer lifetime value?

Those aren't always the same answer.
