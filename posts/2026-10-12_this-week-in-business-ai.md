# This Week in Business AI — October 12, 2026

## Story/Angle
Three stories from this week paint a clear picture of where enterprise AI actually stands: one big adoption signal from Microsoft, a Gartner warning on agentic AI, and McKinsey data on the gap between AI "users" and AI "transformers."

## LinkedIn Post

This week in business AI — here's what stood out:

✅ THE WIN
Microsoft 365 Copilot crossed 30 million paid seats this quarter. Large enterprise deals (50,000+ seats) grew 7x year over year. That's not trial adoption — that's procurement cycles catching up to pilots that already worked. When the finance team signs off on 50k seats, someone showed them real numbers.

⚠️ THE WARNING
Gartner is projecting that 40%+ of agentic AI initiatives will be cancelled before end of 2027. The stated reasons: escalating costs and business value nobody can actually pin down. That's not a technology problem. That's a scoping problem — and it's exactly what happens when you start with the AI and work backwards to a use case.

📈 THE TREND
McKinsey's latest global survey found 75% of AI high performers have fundamentally redesigned their workflows (up from 55% last year). Among everyone else? Just 25%. The gap between "using AI" and "transforming with AI" is widening fast.

---
The pattern is consistent: companies winning with AI didn't bolt it onto existing processes. They rethought the work first. The ones heading for cancellation are still treating AI as a productivity layer on top of broken workflows.
