# This Week in Business AI — 19 October 2026

## Story/Angle
Three stories this week that cut through the noise: a landmark Wharton–GBK ROI study with hard governance numbers, Character.AI's Chapter 11 filing and the data protection scramble it triggered, and AI officially becoming the #1 stated reason for US job cuts in 2026.

## LinkedIn Post

This week in business AI — here's what stood out:

✅ THE WIN
New Wharton–GBK research (released Oct 13) found companies with a dedicated Chief AI Officer report strong ROI 58% of the time — versus just 24% for those tagging AI oversight onto an existing exec. Same old lesson, new data: accountability drives outcomes.

⚠️ THE WARNING
Character.AI — the AI companion platform that reached 20 million monthly users — filed for Chapter 11 this week. State attorneys general are now asking a federal judge to protect children's chat histories from the bankruptcy process. Fast growth, chaotic governance, questionable safety practices. Pick any two.

📈 THE TREND
AI is now the #1 stated reason for announced US job cuts in 2026. Through September: 120,136 job cuts cited as AI-driven — 21% of all announced US cuts. This isn't a prediction anymore. It's in the Challenger report.
